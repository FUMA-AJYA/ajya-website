OUR HISTORY

Building Connections

Youth TICAD 2025 and TICAD Youth Drive 2026 represent important milestones in Africa–Japan youth collaboration. AJYA was officially launched during TICAD Youth Drive 2026 in Accra, Ghana.

A youth dialogue and policy proposal initiative connected to the development of Youth Agenda 2055.

Key milestone

Development of Youth Agenda 2055.

2026

TICAD Youth Drive 2026

13–14 August 2026 | Accra, Ghana

Organized by ADNJ and AAYN with the Government of Ghana, the event brought together young people from Africa and Japan. AJYA was officially launched during the event.

Key milestones

285 in-person youth delegates, the official launch of AJYA and the Accra Declaration.
