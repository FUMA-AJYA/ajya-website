ABOUT AJYA

Connecting Africa and Japan through youth-led collaboration.

OUR STORY

Our Beginning

Africa & Japan Youth Alliance (AJYA) was officially launched during TICAD Youth Drive 2026 in Accra, Ghana. It seeks to strengthen collaboration between young people across Africa and Japan and help turn dialogue into meaningful action.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

01 / MISSION

OUR MISSION

To connect young people across Africa and Japan, foster collaboration and support the transformation of ideas into practical initiatives.

02 / VISION

OUR VISION

A future where young people in Africa and Japan work together to create lasting positive change.

OUR DIRECTION

Youth Agenda 2055

Youth Agenda 2055 provides a framework of three pillars and two cross-cutting enablers. AJYA aims to support youth-led initiatives aligned with this framework.
