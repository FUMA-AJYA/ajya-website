OUR ACTIVITIES

OUR APPROACH

From Dialogue to Action

The Africa & Japan Youth Alliance (AJYA) aims to connect young people across Africa and Japan, encourage collaboration and support youth-led initiatives.

HOW WE WORK

Areas of Action

The following are proposed areas of action for AJYA. Specific programs and implementation plans will be developed as the alliance grows.

01

CONNECT

Build relationships among young people, organizations and partners in Africa and Japan.

02

COLLABORATE

Create opportunities for youth to exchange ideas, share experience and work together.

03

TAKE ACTION

Support the development of youth-led ideas into practical initiatives with long-term impact.
